## 2. MEDICAL CONSULTATION IS ESSENTIAL

### 2.1 Always Consult a Qualified Healthcare Provider
You are **strongly advised** to consult a licensed physician, neurologist, or qualified healthcare professional:
- Before starting, stopping, or changing any medication or treatment
- If you are experiencing a new type of headache you have not had before
- If your existing headache pattern has changed significantly
- Before making significant changes to your lifestyle based on information from this Service
- If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant
- If you have a history of cardiovascular disease, stroke, or other neurological conditions
- If you are a minor (under 18 years of age)

### 2.2 Seek Specialist Care for Diagnosis
Migraine must be **diagnosed by a qualified medical professional** using established clinical criteria (ICHD-3). This chatbot cannot diagnose migraine, distinguish migraine from other types of headache disorders, or rule out other potentially serious neurological conditions.

### 2.3 Do Not Self-Medicate Based on Chatbot Output
This Service may provide general educational information about categories of medications used for migraine. This information is **strictly educational**. Do not start, stop, or alter any medication regimen based on this chatbot's output without consulting your doctor or pharmacist.

## 3. EMERGENCY SITUATIONS — SEEK IMMEDIATE HELP

### 3.1 This Service Cannot Handle Medical Emergencies
**This chatbot is not equipped to manage medical emergencies.** Do not rely on this Service in situations requiring immediate medical attention.

### 3.2 Call Emergency Services Immediately If You Experience:
- A sudden, severe headache described as the **"worst headache of your life"** (possible sign of subarachnoid hemorrhage)
- Headache accompanied by **fever, stiff neck, sensitivity to light** (possible signs of meningitis)
- Headache with **sudden vision loss, slurred speech, facial drooping, or arm/leg weakness** (possible signs of stroke or TIA)
- Headache following a **head injury or trauma**
- Headache with **confusion, loss of consciousness, or altered mental status**
- Headache in a **person over 50 with no prior headache history**
- **Continuous vomiting** that prevents you from keeping fluids down
- **Visual disturbances or neurological symptoms** that do not resolve

**In India:** Call **112** (National Emergency Number)  
**In the United States:** Call **911**  
**In the European Union:** Call **112**  
**Or go directly to your nearest Emergency Department.**
